## Revision summary

UK payments regulation splits between the FCA (conduct) and the PRA (prudential soundness, part of the Bank of England), implemented in practice through the Payment Services Regulations (PSD2-style rules on authorisation, Strong Customer Authentication, complaints, and liability), alongside industry standards like PCI DSS and evolving APP fraud reimbursement rules. Emerging payment technologies span global instant payment schemes (UPI, Pix, FedNow/RTP), Request to Pay, Buy Now Pay Later (drawing growing regulatory scrutiny), and embedded finance delivered via APIs and Banking-as-a-Service. Digital currencies range from cryptocurrencies (decentralised, often volatile) to stablecoins (pegged, reserve-backed) to CBDCs (centrally issued, risk-free), with central banks including the Bank of England actively exploring a potential digital pound. Payments Operations in practice means monitoring exception queues, resolving issues within SLAs, and escalating appropriately, measured through KPIs like STP rate. And the full CPCM syllabus opens onto genuinely varied career pathways — Payments Operations, Treasury, Transaction Banking, Compliance/Financial Crime, and Product/Innovation — all resting on the same foundational knowledge this course has built from lesson one onward. This completes all 40 lessons of the CPCM curriculum.

## Flashcards

??? question "FCA vs PRA?"
    The FCA regulates conduct; the PRA, part of the Bank of England, regulates the prudential soundness of larger firms.

??? question "What do the Payment Services Regulations implement?"
    PSD2-style requirements into UK law.

??? question "Name three global instant payment schemes beyond the UK/EU."
    UPI (India), Pix (Brazil), and FedNow/RTP (US).

??? question "What does Request to Pay give the payer that a Direct Debit doesn't?"
    Visibility and choice over each individual payment request before authorising it.

??? question "Why is BNPL facing growing regulatory attention?"
    Concerns about consumer over-indebtedness and inadequate affordability checks.

??? question "Cryptocurrency vs stablecoin vs CBDC?"
    Cryptocurrency is decentralised and often volatile; a stablecoin is pegged and privately backed; a CBDC is centrally issued by a central bank and risk-free.

??? question "What is a key risk consideration for retail CBDCs?"
    The potential impact on commercial bank deposits and lending capacity.

??? question "What is an SLA?"
    An agreed target timeframe or standard for processing or resolving a task.

??? question "What is STP rate an example of?"
    A KPI measuring straight-through processing performance.

??? question "Name five common CPCM-aligned career pathways."
    Payments Operations, Treasury, Transaction Banking, Compliance/Financial Crime, and Product/Innovation.

??? question "What is PCI DSS, in terms of who mandates it?"
    An industry-mandated card data security standard, not a piece of government legislation.

??? question "What does a strong scenario-question exam answer include beyond the final answer?"
    The reasoning and application of the relevant concept to the specific scenario, not just a one-word answer.
